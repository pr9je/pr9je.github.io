About me

Engineer who thinks in data.

I spent two years at Blue Star Ltd. building and validating VRF HVAC systems — running thermal fluid models, correlating simulation outputs with test data, and communicating findings to product teams. That experience taught me something most data people never develop: physical intuition for what data means.

Now I'm channelling that into Data Analytics and AI/ML. I'm pursuing my MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (Woolf University, EU) while actively building skills in Python, SQL, Tableau, and machine learning. My goal is roles at the intersection of engineering and intelligence — industrial IoT, energy systems, predictive analytics, product data science.

I believe the best data analysts aren't just tool-users — they're domain-fluent problem solvers. That's what I'm building toward.
